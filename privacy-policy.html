Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes the current data practices of BERSHA GAMES for Block Merge Rush. Contact: bershagames@gmail.com.

1. Introduction

Block Merge Rush is currently designed to work without a user account or a BERSHA GAMES backend service.

2. Information We Collect

The current version does not transmit personal data to BERSHA GAMES servers. It does not require a name, email address, location, contacts, government identifier, or account registration.

3. Local Device Data

Game progress, settings, achievements and related game state are stored locally on the user's device. A player nickname, if entered, is also stored locally only.

4. Data Sharing

BERSHA GAMES does not currently receive or share Block Merge Rush player data with third parties because the current app does not transmit that data to BERSHA GAMES.

5. Advertising

The current version does not integrate Google AdMob or another advertising SDK.

6. In-App Purchases

The current version does not integrate Google Play Billing or another billing SDK. No active in-app purchase system is provided in this version.

7. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current version does not integrate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

8. Children's Privacy

Block Merge Rush is not specifically designed for children. BERSHA GAMES does not knowingly collect personal information through the current version of Block Merge Rush.

9. Data Security

Because current game data remains on the device and is not sent to BERSHA GAMES servers, users should protect access to their device. Standard device functionality such as vibration may be used when enabled by the user.

10. Data Retention

Local game data remains on the device until it is reset or cleared by the user, removed through Android app-data controls, or removed by uninstalling the app, subject to Android device backup behavior.

11. User Choices and Data Deletion

Users can reset or delete local game data from within the app where available, clear Block Merge Rush storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. See the Data Deletion page.

12. Future Feature Changes

If advertising, billing, Google Play Games Services, cloud save, analytics, crash reporting, social login or similar services are added later, this policy will be updated before or alongside those features as appropriate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BERSHA GAMES may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when appropriate.

14. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be sent to bershagames@gmail.com.
